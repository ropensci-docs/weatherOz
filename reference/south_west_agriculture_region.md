# Western Australia Southwest Agriculture Region Geospatial Polygon

An [sf](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=sf) object of the the WA
South West Agricultural Region.

## Usage

``` r
south_west_agricultural_region
```

## Format

An [`sf::sf()`](https://r-spatial.github.io/sf/reference/sf.html)
polygon object

## Source

[Western Australia Department of Primary Industries and Regional
Development](https://catalogue.data.wa.gov.au/dataset/south-west-agricultural-region-dpird-008)
under a [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
Licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

## Details

The zone managed for intensive agricultural activities in South-Western
Australia. Also known as the South West Agricultural Area or Clearing
Line. This zone defines the easternmost extent of land cleared for
agricultural purposes.

## Base data sets

Western Australian Land Information Authority - Captured from
photographic interpretation of best available orthophotography at date
of capture, dates range between 2007 and 2010.

## Scale of capture

1:20,000

## Coordinate Reference System

EPSG:4326 - WGS 84 – WGS84 - World Geodetic System 1984, used in GPS
<https://epsg.io/4326>
